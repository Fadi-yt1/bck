    FAQ

    Good questions

    Which file types can I upload?

    PNG, JPG, WebP and HEIC, up to 15 MB per image. Very large photos are resized to 2500 px on the longest edge before processing, which keeps things fast without a visible drop in quality.

    Do you keep my images?

    No. Images are streamed straight through to the processing API and returned to your browser. Nothing is written to disk or to a database on our side, and the cutouts you see live only in your browser tab until you close it.

    Is there a usage limit?

    There's an hourly cap per visitor so that one person can't exhaust the service for everyone else. If you hit it, the app tells you roughly how long to wait.

    Why is the background swap instant but the cutout isn't?

    Removing the background is the part that needs the AI model, so it's a round trip to the server. Once you have the cutout, everything else — colours, backdrops, format changes — is composited on a canvas in your browser, so it's immediate and doesn't use up your quota.

    Can I use the results commercially?

    The cutouts are yours. Just make sure you have the rights to the image you upload in the first place.
